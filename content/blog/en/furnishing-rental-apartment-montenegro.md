The Montenegrin coast has many apartments for short- and long-term rental. Most are furnished the same way: flat-pack furniture, a kitchen with too little storage, and a wardrobe that does not match how guests actually use the space. Guests notice. Ratings drop. Owners wonder why.

We furnish apartments for owners along the Bar–Budva–Tivat coast. Here are the mistakes we see repeatedly and what works better.

## Mistake 1: buying furniture before measuring

Standard cabinets often do not fit a kitchen wall without awkward fillers. Montenegrin apartments — especially in older buildings — have irregular walls, pipes in odd places, and ceiling heights that vary across a room. Custom-built furniture uses the real geometry, so space for an ironing board, vacuum and suitcase rack does not have to be improvised later.

## Mistake 2: no entry storage

Guests arrive with suitcases, shoes and wet jackets. Without an entry wardrobe or shoe cabinet, these items end up on the floor of the bedroom. A built-in entry wall solves this with hanging space, shoe storage and a shelf for keys and chargers.

## Mistake 3: the wrong kitchen worktop

Guests are not as careful as owners. Hot pans go directly on the surface, red wine sits overnight, and cleaning happens with whatever is under the sink. The worktop should be selected for real use, not only for how it looks on a sample.

## Mistake 4: ignoring the balcony

A furnished balcony can change the way an apartment looks in listing photos. Yet many owners leave the balcony empty or put out a plastic chair from the supermarket. Compact weather-resistant seating often makes the space feel more complete without complicating the whole project.

## What a practical furnishing package looks like

For a rental apartment, we usually review the kitchen, entry storage, bedroom wardrobe and bathroom vanity together. Scope and timing are confirmed after the floor plan, measurement and materials are reviewed. The owner gets one specification and furniture built to the actual walls — not to a catalogue drawing.
